### Pretty easy to operate: 
You just need to buy all parts from the BOM list  and order the pcb board. 

## for 5 Volts leds 
find working  5V DC power suplly and conect to screw terminals 

The bottom  terminal are for powering  addressable led strip. 
<br> 
And don't forget to connect signal cable from the signal pad   best to use shielding cable for less noise then use small via to connect one end of shield in the cable
